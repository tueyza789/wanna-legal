Common questions

How does it work? Post a plan (“Wanna grab coffee tomorrow?”) or tap I’m in on someone else’s. The host picks one person, and a private chat opens with the plan already set.

Why didn’t I hear back? Hosts pick one person per plan. If someone else was picked, you’ll get a gentle heads-up — keep scrolling, there’s always another plan.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Settings → Delete account.
